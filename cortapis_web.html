ℹ️ How It Works

Step 1: Your text is marked with @ symbols in Markdown format to identify sensitive content.
Step 2: Text is obfuscated with scattered @ markers to confuse alphabetical search patterns.
Step 3: Content is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using your password as the key.
Step 4: Encrypted text is safe to upload to cloud services without AI indexing concerns.
Step 5: Open the "call window" to decrypt and view content (when indexing won't occur).
